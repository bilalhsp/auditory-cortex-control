# Auditory cortex control

An application for fitting neural encoding models and generating audio stimuli
for recorded neural responses.

## Language

**Application session**:
An operator's work period grouping computations and their generated results.
_Avoid_: Recording session when referring to operator activity

**Recording session**:
A collection of recorded neural responses associated with presented stimuli,
uploaded or selected for model fitting.
_Avoid_: Application session when referring to recorded data

**Generated sample**:
An audio stimulus produced by a generation task targeting a chosen neural unit
and objective.

**Session history**:
The saved collection of previous application sessions and their retained results.
